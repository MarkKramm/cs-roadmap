# When to Buy TryHackMe Premium

## The recommendation

Buy **one month only around Cybersecurity Phase 4–5**, not during foundations. The exact price and included content can change, so verify the current price on the official site before subscribing.

## Trigger for purchase

Buy only when all of these are true:

- [ ] You completed Cyber Phase 1 foundations.
- [ ] You can use Linux shell for basic files, permissions, processes, and logs.
- [ ] You understand IP, DNS, TCP/UDP, HTTP, and common ports.
- [ ] You completed at least 10 free rooms or equivalent free labs.
- [ ] You have a working study schedule.
- [ ] You can commit 20–30 focused hours during one month.
- [ ] You know whether you are prioritizing SOC/Blue Team or pentest/web.

## What to complete before buying

- TryHackMe free Pre Security or equivalent fundamentals
- Linux Journey basics
- OverTheWire Bandit levels 0–10
- At least 5 PortSwigger Apprentice labs
- At least 1 incident report or blue-team lab report
- Basic Nmap and Wireshark practice
- A portfolio folder for notes and screenshots

## Prioritize during the paid month

Choose a single primary route rather than clicking random rooms.

### If targeting SOC/Blue Team

Prioritize currently available rooms/paths covering:

- SOC fundamentals
- Windows/Linux logging
- SIEM concepts and alert triage
- Phishing analysis
- Incident response
- Network traffic analysis
- Splunk or similar log search
- MITRE ATT&CK mapping

### If targeting pentest/web

Prioritize currently available rooms/paths covering:

- Web application fundamentals
- Burp Suite
- OWASP Top 10
- Nmap and enumeration
- Linux privilege concepts
- Basic exploitation in legal labs
- Reporting and remediation

Room names and paths change. Use the current TryHackMe catalog and select rooms that produce skills/reports, not only flags.

## 30-day schedule

| Days | Focus | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Pick path, plan calendar, read room prerequisites | One-month plan |
| 3–7 | Complete foundational rooms in chosen path | Notes + screenshots |
| 8–14 | Complete core tool rooms: SIEM/logs or web/Burp | Tool cheat sheet |
| 15–21 | Complete practical investigations/boxes | 2 lab reports |
| 22–26 | Complete harder rooms and revisit weak topics | Gap list |
| 27–28 | Build one portfolio project from lab learning | Draft report |
| 29 | Review notes and practice interview explanations | 10 Q&A |
| 30 | Export/save notes, cancel/downgrade if desired | Final portfolio update |

Suggested daily block: **60–90 minutes**, 5–6 days/week. Stop when focus is gone; more hours do not automatically mean more learning.

## After the month ends

- Downgrade to the free plan if you do not need Premium.
- Save your own notes, screenshots, commands, and reports; do not copy room answers.
- Continue with Wazuh, PortSwigger, CyberDefenders, BTLO, LetsDefend, OverTheWire, and local VMs.
- Turn at least 3 completed rooms into portfolio write-ups.
- Do not buy another subscription until you finish the current projects.

## Warning against buying too early

Premium cannot fix weak fundamentals, inconsistent study, or lack of documentation. If you buy before you can understand the rooms, you may spend the month clicking through guided tasks and retain very little. Free resources are enough to reach the purchase trigger.

## Budget note

This is optional. If $14 is not comfortable, do not buy it. A completed Wazuh project plus free labs is stronger than an abandoned paid subscription.
