# Phase 7 — Certifications

## Goal of this phase

Choose certifications strategically instead of buying certificates as a substitute for skills or projects.

## Estimated time

**4–12 weeks optional**, depending on the certification and your job situation.

## Skills you'll gain

- Compare certification value, cost, and role alignment.
- Build a study plan without abandoning hands-on labs.
- Decide when a certification is worth your limited money.
- Explain certifications honestly on your resume.

## Recommended order

### First choice: no-cost proof

Before paying for an exam, complete:

- IT portfolio
- Cyber portfolio
- Networking/Linux labs
- Wazuh or equivalent SIEM practice
- Incident report
- Job applications

### Possible entry-level certifications

- CompTIA A+: useful for IT support, but exam costs money; not required for every job.
- CompTIA Network+: useful for networking/NOC, but exam costs money.
- CompTIA Security+: widely recognized baseline; valuable for many security job filters, but exam costs money.
- ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC): may have free training/exam offers depending on current program availability; verify current terms.
- Google Cybersecurity Certificate: structured but paid certificate; free audit/course content may vary.
- Microsoft SC-900: beginner security/compliance/identity exam; exam fee applies, free training exists.
- AZ-900: cloud fundamentals; useful if cloud roles appear in your target market.

## Hands-on practice tasks

1. Read the official objectives for one certification.
2. Mark each topic green/yellow/red.
3. Use free training to close red gaps.
4. Do not schedule an exam until you can explain the objectives and have practical proof.
5. Compare the certification against 30 target job posts.
6. Ask: does this cert remove a real hiring filter for my target role?

## Free vs Paid

### What's free and enough

Professor Messer, Microsoft Learn, Cisco Skills for All, official objectives, and free labs are enough to build skills.

### What's paid and why you'd upgrade

Exam fees buy a standardized credential that can pass HR filters. They do not replace experience or projects.

### When it's worth paying

Prioritize Security+ or ISC2 CC only after your IT income/job search is stable or an employer requires it. Do not borrow money for a cert just to feel progress.
