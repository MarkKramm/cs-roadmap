# Cybersecurity Roadmap Overview

## Goal

Build from vague basics to **entry-level cyber employability** in 6–18 months while pursuing or working an IT job.

Target roles:

- SOC Analyst L1
- Junior IT Security Analyst
- Junior GRC Analyst
- Junior Pentest Trainee
- Security Support Analyst

## Hiring Reality

Cybersecurity is not just hacking. Entry-level cyber hiring usually rewards:

- Networking fundamentals
- Linux and Windows confidence
- Log reading
- Clear reports
- Basic scripting
- Security tools used safely
- Evidence-based thinking
- IT support experience

## Timeline

| Phase | Topic | Realistic Time |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Foundations | 4 weeks |
| 2 | Networking and Linux | 6 weeks |
| 3 | Security fundamentals | 6 weeks |
| 4 | Hands-on labs | 8–12 weeks |
| 5 | Specialization choice | 4 weeks |

| 7 | Certifications | optional 4–12 weeks |

Total: **6–18 months**. If working full-time, expect the longer side.

## Cyber Lab Safety Rules

- Only scan systems you own or have explicit permission to test.
- Use TryHackMe, PortSwigger, CyberDefenders, local VMs, or intentionally vulnerable machines.
- Do not attack public IPs, school/work networks, neighbors, or random websites.
- Keep notes of what you did and why.

## Portfolio Target

By the time you apply, aim for 3–5 strong projects:

1. Wazuh SIEM home lab with Windows/Linux logs and 3 custom rules
2. Wireshark network traffic analysis report
3. Web vulnerability write-up from PortSwigger labs
4. Incident report from CyberDefenders/BTLO/LetsDefend
5. GRC risk register and policy mini-pack or pentest report depending on specialization
